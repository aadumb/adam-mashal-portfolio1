A community-centered mobile platform connecting Alaska veterans with trusted resources, employment opportunities, peer support, and local community.

Flutter Firebase Firestore Mobile UX Community Platform

Project Overview

Connection should not depend on knowing where to look.

Vet & Net is being built to make support more visible, accessible, and practical for veterans living across Alaska. Rather than searching through disconnected websites, social groups, and outdated directories, members can find useful information in one focused platform.

The application combines community spaces, local resources, employment opportunities, direct messaging, and personalized profile preferences into one secure mobile experience.

Core Experience

Built around
real connection.

01

Community Spaces

Public and private rooms give veterans a place to connect, share information, ask questions, and build trusted local support networks.

02

Clear access to veteran services, benefits guidance, community assistance, and location-aware support.

03

Personalized Profiles

Members can manage preferences, saved opportunities, communication settings, and their community identity.

Design Principle

“Make essential support feel as approachable as sending a message.”
